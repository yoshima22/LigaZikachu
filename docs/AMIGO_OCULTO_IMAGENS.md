# Amigo Oculto — imagens da cartinha (prompts para o Codex)

Ilustração do topo da cartinha: 1024×1024, **fundo transparente**, personagem(ns) centralizado(s) com contorno branco grosso estilo adesivo, sem texto. Depois de gerar: converter para webp, subir em `assets/events/amigo-oculto/` e preencher `url` do item em `IMAGES` (`src/lib/amigo-oculto/cards.ts`).

## Bloco de estilo (colar no início de TODOS os prompts)
> Cartoon game-art sticker illustration, thick clean outlines, saturated festive colors, soft rim light, funny expressive faces, square 1:1 1024x1024, transparent background, subject centered with a thick white sticker outline, Christmas theme (red, gold, pine green). Keep each character's face, hair, outfit and colors EXACTLY as in its reference image. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no background scenery.

## As 10 cenas
1. **Noel preso na chaminé (Jotape)** — Jotape (ref: [LINK_JOTAPE]) dressed as Santa, stuck upside-down in a brick chimney with only the legs and the red hat kicking out, a sack of gifts spilling, one gift falling.
2. **Enguiça e a lista do Noel (Professor Enguiça)** — Professor Enguiça (ref: professor-enguica) holding a giant scroll "naughty list" that unrolls to the floor, a satisfied evil grin, a tiny Santa hat, a pencil behind the ear.
3. **Cleide acende a árvore (Dra. Cleide)** — Dra. Cleide Combustão (ref: dra-cleide) in her lab coat and goggles, a Christmas tree behind her blowing up in harmless colorful sparks and smoke, her hair singed, proud smile, holding a test tube with glowing red-green liquid.
4. **Miauvadão derruba a árvore (Miauvadão)** — Miauvadão the cat (ref: miauvadao-cat) at the top of a toppling mini Christmas tree, pushing a bauble off with one paw while staring at the camera with zero regret, baubles rolling around.
5. **Papai Noel Miauvadão (Miauvadão)** — Miauvadão wearing a too-big Santa hat over his eyes and a fake white beard, sitting on a pile of gift boxes, sleepy and unimpressed, a coin purse on his lap.
6. **Amigo oculto suspeito (Jotape + Miauvadão)** — Jotape and Miauvadão hiding behind a Christmas tree with fake mustaches and sunglasses, peeking out, each holding a gift behind the back, comically suspicious.
7. **Laboratório natalino (Cleide + Enguiça)** — Dra. Cleide and Professor Enguiça side by side at a lab table wrapping a gift in a fire-resistant blanket, a small flame on the ribbon, Enguiça panicking and Cleide calm giving a thumbs-up.
8. **Troca de presentes (todos)** — Jotape, Professor Enguiça, Dra. Cleide and Miauvadão in a row, each holding a wrapped gift and looking at the neighbor's gift with envy, Santa hats, cheerful chaos.
9. **Ceia da Liga (todos)** — the four characters around a table with a panettone and a pavê, Miauvadão on the table eating the cake, Jotape holding a fork like a sword toward Enguiça, Cleide separating them.
10. **Brinde de fim de ano (todos)** — the four characters raising cups of eggnog in a toast, confetti and golden stars, Miauvadão wearing a tiny party hat on the table, everyone laughing.

## Como usar
Cole o **bloco de estilo** + a cena + anexe as referências citadas. Gere uma imagem por cena, envie os arquivos e eu converto para webp, publico no Storage e ligo cada uma na lista de ilustrações da cartinha.
